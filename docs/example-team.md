# Beispielteam

Fünf fiktive Personen: Lena, Mila und Sara (weiblich), Noah und Jonas (männlich).

Generiert mit dem integrierten Imagegen-Tool. Die PNGs besitzen einen transparenten Hintergrund und wurden unverändert kopiert. Die Bilder sind Beispielmaterial, keine Fotos echter Teammitglieder.

## Generierungsprompts

### lena

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: individual example team avatar for a customer service portal.
Primary request: Create exactly ONE photorealistic fictional team member portrait, isolated as a clean cutout on a genuinely transparent background with alpha.
Subject: Fictional woman aged about 32, fair skin, shoulder-length chestnut hair, friendly natural smile, navy blue crew-neck blouse.
Composition/framing: square canvas, centered front-facing head-and-shoulders portrait, full head and hair visible, equal generous clear margin above and beside the hair, shoulders visible, upper chest reaching bottom canvas edge, eye level camera, matching professional team headshot composition.
Lighting/mood: soft neutral studio light, approachable and professional, natural realistic skin texture, sharp eyes, subtle relaxed smile.
Constraints: only one person; real alpha transparency everywhere outside the person including hair edges; clean fine hair cutout; no background, backdrop, gradient, scene, circle, border, text, logo, watermark, props, headset or shadow on the transparent area. Do not draw a checkerboard. No overly airbrushed skin.
```
